## Items

### pH Indicator

first, boil beetroot in a crucible. then, crush paper into paper strips using the mortar and pestle. finally, soak those paper strips in the beetroot juice to get minimalistic pH indicators. (in real life, you'd use red cabbage or otherwise)

this can be used to monitor the pH of your soil by right clicking on it, so your plants wont rot or turn yellow

### Fertilizer

regular bonemeal cant be used for plants, right? so for herbalis herbs you need to craft fertilizer using **compost**, **bone meal** and **ash**.

### Compost

this is one of the ingredients for fertilizer, which will be obtained from either letting your herbs decompose, or maybe use the composter(?)

### Ash

when anything wooden burns, it releases ash and they fall like snow layers (inspired by the Create modpack CABIN). they can be destroyed to get ash, which is your primary alkali source. this can be used as fertilizer, or soil pH regulation

### Sulfur

you can find sulfur in yellow toxic pools at the surface, or as crystallic sulfur deep in caves in small patches. this isnt used for much other than acidifying your soil (again, pH regulation), or oddly specific recipes
